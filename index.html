      About and Legal

      Welcome to All The Mastery Points, or ATMP for short! All The Mastery Points is a website that archives pro players' and streamers' accounts, in order to calculate and showcase their total mastery points.

      You can also do your own calculations with your own account, and mark them as favorite, so you don't have to look yourself or someone else up in the future.

      How and who can be added

      If you want to suggest who to add to the list of high profile players, send their socials in #who-to-add on our Discord server, like their Twitch channel or Twitter account, and we will do our research to add them. You can aslo DM LxVer8 if you don't want to join the server.

      If the person you want added to the website is on this server, or it's even yourself, open a ticket and we will get in contact with you to add them or you.

      If you:

      • Wish to become a Helper, open a ticket or DM me. Your job would be to find / verify that someone's account is actually theirs, or if a streamer you know has a new account.
      • Want to be removed from the High Profile Players, or remove an account of yours, open a ticket or DM LxVer8 on Discord.
      • Found your account on someone else's profile, open a ticket or DM LxVer8 on Discord.
      • Want to customize your profile by changing the picture or name, open a ticket or DM LxVer8 on Discord.

      Quick reminder:

      We will accept any kind of account to the website, even if it was permanently banned or from the PBE. As long as you have the name and ID of the account, most accounts stay available for the API.

      We will, however, not accept accounts in which you play that are not mainly yours. For example, if you boosted someone's account. That account will not be added, since it is not directly yours.

      And just as a reminder, neither me nor ATMP are affiliated to Riot Games at all. We will NEVER ask for access to your account whatsoever; it is not needed in order to legitimize that an account is yours. If someone does reach out to you and asks for any information, please block them, then open a ticket, and report them to us.

      If, for whatever reason, Riot Games decides to use this website to find people sharing accounts (which I doubt they will anyway), neither me nor ATMP is responsible for whatever can happen to your account.

      ATMP is VERY early in development, and it can take literally MONTHS for Riot Games to provide the API key needed to move the project to the next level.
